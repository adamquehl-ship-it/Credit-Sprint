FAQ

Questions students ask

Is Credit Sprint affiliated with the College Board?

No. Credit Sprint is an independent study tool. AP® and CLEP® are trademarks of the College Board, which doesn't endorse this site. Every practice question is written by Credit Sprint.

Does it work for both AP® and CLEP®?

Yes. Switch exams any time. The practice exam length, unit weights, score estimate and study plan all change to match.

How accurate is the score prediction?

It's a rough estimate based on your recent answers in each unit, weighted like the real exam. Use it to see whether you're trending up, not as an official score.

Do I need an account?

Not right now. Your progress is saved in your browser. Accounts that sync across devices are coming soon.

How much time do I need?

Ten to fifteen minutes a day is enough to make steady progress. With less than a month to go, switch on the 30-day cram plan.
