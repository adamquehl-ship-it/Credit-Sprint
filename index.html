Nine courses, sixteen exams

Sixteen ways to earn college credit

AP® and CLEP® cover the same core material in each course. Study once and you're ready for both. If May doesn't go your way, CLEP® is a second shot. Marketing is a CLEP® exam only, World History is an AP® exam only, and U.S. History pairs AP® with the two CLEP® U.S. History exams.

Macroeconomics

GDP, inflation, money and banking, fiscal and monetary policy, international trade.

  • AP®: 60 multiple-choice questions in 70 minutes, then 3 free-response questions
  • CLEP®: about 80 multiple-choice questions in 90 minutes; a 50 earns credit at many colleges
  • Units: Basics · Indicators · AD–AS · Money & banks · Long run · Open economy
  • Signature games: Fed Chair, Shift It, Chain Reaction
Study Macroeconomics

Microeconomics

Supply and demand, elasticity, costs, perfect competition, monopoly, game theory, labor markets, externalities.

  • AP®: 60 multiple-choice questions in 70 minutes, then 3 free-response questions
  • CLEP®: about 80 multiple-choice questions in 90 minutes; a 50 earns credit at many colleges
  • Units: Basics · Supply & demand · Costs & competition · Imperfect competition · Factor markets · Market failure
  • Signature games: Profit Max, Nash Hunt, Shift It
Study Microeconomics

Biology

Biochemistry, cells, energy, the cell cycle, genetics, gene expression, evolution and ecology, plus CLEP® organismal biology.

  • AP®: 60 multiple-choice questions in 70 minutes, then 3 free-response questions
  • CLEP®: about 80 multiple-choice questions in 90 minutes; a 50 earns credit at many colleges
  • Units: Chemistry of life · Cells · Energetics · Signaling & cycle · Heredity · Gene expression · Evolution · Ecology
  • Signature games: Cross It, Chain Reaction, Number Crunch
Study Biology

Chemistry

Atomic structure, bonding, intermolecular forces, reactions, kinetics, thermodynamics, equilibrium, acids and bases, electrochemistry.

  • AP®: 60 multiple-choice questions in 70 minutes, then 3 free-response questions
  • CLEP®: about 80 multiple-choice questions in 90 minutes; a 50 earns credit at many colleges
  • Units: Atomic structure · Bonding · Intermolecular forces · Chemical reactions · Kinetics · Thermodynamics · Equilibrium · Acids and bases · Entropy and electrochemistry
  • Signature games: Balance It, Chain Reaction, Number Crunch
Study Chemistry

Marketing

Marketing and society, the marketing concept, research, consumer behavior, segmentation and positioning, B2B, products and brands, pricing, distribution, promotion and e-commerce.

  • CLEP®: about 80 multiple-choice questions in 90 minutes; a 50 earns credit at many colleges
  • Units: Society and ethics · Concept and strategy · Environment and research · Consumer behavior · Segmentation (STP) · B2B markets · Product and brand · Pricing · Place and channels · Promotion and online
  • Signature games: Mix Master, Perceptual Map, Number Crunch
Study Marketing

U.S. History

Contact and colonization, the Revolution, the early republic, reform and expansion, the Civil War and Reconstruction, the Gilded Age, the world wars, the Cold War and civil rights, and today. AP® U.S. History plus CLEP® History of the U.S. I and II.

  • AP®: 55 multiple-choice questions in 55 minutes, then 3 short-answer questions, a DBQ and a long essay
  • CLEP®: about 120 multiple-choice questions in 90 minutes; a 50 earns credit at many colleges
  • Units: Contact (1491–1607) · Colonial America · Revolution and Nation · Expansion and Reform · Civil War and Reconstruction · Gilded Age · Progressivism to WWII · Cold War and Civil Rights · Modern America
  • Signature games: Timeline Sort, Source Match, Chain Reaction
Study U.S. History

World History

Global tapestry and trade networks, land-based empires, the Atlantic world, revolutions, industrialization, the world wars, the Cold War and decolonization, and globalization. AP® World History: Modern only.

  • AP®: 55 multiple-choice questions in 55 minutes, then 3 short-answer questions, a DBQ and a long essay
  • Units: Global Tapestry · Networks of Exchange · Land-Based Empires · Transoceanic Links · Revolutions · Industrialization · Global Conflict · Cold War and Decolonization · Globalization
  • Signature games: Timeline Sort, Source Match, Chain Reaction
Study World History

U.S. Government

Foundations of democracy, Congress, the presidency and the courts, civil liberties and civil rights, political beliefs and elections, with landmark cases and foundational documents. AP® U.S. Government and Politics plus CLEP® American Government.

  • AP®: 60 multiple-choice questions in 70 minutes, then 3 free-response questions
  • CLEP®: about 80 multiple-choice questions in 90 minutes; a 50 earns credit at many colleges
  • Units: Foundations · Branches · Rights · Ideologies · Participation
  • Signature games: Court Case Match, Branch Check, Amendment Sprint
Study U.S. Government

Psychology

Biological bases of behavior, cognition, development and learning, social psychology and personality, mental and physical health, with famous studies and research methods. AP® Psychology plus CLEP® Introductory Psychology.

  • AP®: 60 multiple-choice questions in 70 minutes, then 3 free-response questions
  • CLEP®: about 80 multiple-choice questions in 90 minutes; a 50 earns credit at many colleges
  • Units: Biology · Cognition · Dev & Learning · Social & Personality · Health
  • Signature games: Famous Studies Match, Brain Check, Disorder Sort
Study Psychology

FAQ

Questions students ask

Is Credit Sprint affiliated with the College Board?

No. Credit Sprint is an independent study tool. AP® and CLEP® are trademarks of the College Board, which doesn't endorse this site. Every practice question is written by Credit Sprint.

Can I study more than one course at the same time?

Yes. Switch courses any time from the top of the dashboard. Each course keeps its own scores and study plan, while your streak, XP, badges and pets carry over.

Does it work for both AP® and CLEP®?

Yes. Switch exams any time. The practice exam length, unit weights, score estimate and study plan all change to match.

How accurate is the score prediction?

It's a rough estimate based on your recent answers in each unit, weighted like the real exam. Use it to see whether you're trending up, not as an official score.

Do I need an account?

You can try any course right away as a guest, but guest progress is erased when you close the tab. To save your progress and join weekly leagues, create a free account with an email code, Google or a username. Anything you did as a guest carries into your new account.

What do I get on the free plan?

Every unit study guide, the diagnostic and study plan, 3 quizzes a day, mini practice exams, the Daily Challenge, flashcards, pets and weekly leagues. Full practice exams, unlimited quizzes, most games, the mistakes notebook, every FRQ and the 30-day cram plan need a paid plan.

Exam Pass or monthly?

Most AP® students should get the Exam Pass: one payment covers one class through exam day, with nothing to cancel. Monthly plans suit CLEP® students working at their own pace or anyone studying several classes (All subjects is $14.99 a month).

How much time do I need?

Ten to fifteen minutes a day is enough to make steady progress. With less than a month to go, switch on the 30-day cram plan.
