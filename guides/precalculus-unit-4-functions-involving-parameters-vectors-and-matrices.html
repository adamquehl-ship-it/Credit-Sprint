Credit Sprint's Guide to AP® Precalculus, Unit 4: Functions Involving Parameters, Vectors, and Matrices

AP® not on the examCLEP® about 10%

Unit 4 is part of the AP® Precalculus course but is not tested on the AP® exam. It prepares you for calculus and for the analytic geometry on CLEP® Precalculus: parametric functions and motion, conic sections and implicit curves, vectors, and matrices as linear transformations.

What you need to know

  • A parametric function gives x and y as separate functions of a parameter t: (x(t), y(t)). It can describe a particle's position over time and trace curves that are not functions of x.
  • Eliminating the parameter turns parametric equations into an equation in x and y, but loses the direction and timing of the motion.
  • x(t) = cos t, y(t) = sin t traces the unit circle counterclockwise. x = x₀ + at, y = y₀ + bt traces a line.
  • Conic sections: a circle (x − h)² + (y − k)² = r²; an ellipse x²/a² + y²/b² = 1; a hyperbola x²/a² − y²/b² = 1 with asymptotes y = ±(b/a)x; a parabola y = a(x − h)² + k. Complete the square to put an equation in standard form.
  • A vector has magnitude and direction. v = ⟨a, b⟩ has magnitude ‖v‖ = √(a² + b²). Add vectors component by component; multiply by a scalar to stretch or reverse them.
  • The dot product u · v = u₁v₁ + u₂v₂ = ‖u‖‖v‖cos θ. Vectors are perpendicular exactly when u · v = 0.
  • A matrix is a rectangular array. To multiply, take each row of the first times each column of the second. Matrix multiplication is not commutative.
  • For a 2 × 2 matrix [[a, b], [c, d]], the determinant is ad − bc. The inverse exists only if the determinant is not 0: it is (1/(ad − bc))·[[d, −b], [−c, a]].
  • A 2 × 2 matrix represents a linear transformation of the plane: it can rotate, reflect, stretch or shear. |det A| is the factor by which areas change.
  • Transition (Markov) matrices model populations moving between states. Multiplying the state vector by the matrix gives the next state.

Formulas

  • ‖⟨a, b⟩‖ = √(a² + b²)
  • u · v = u₁v₁ + u₂v₂ = ‖u‖‖v‖cos θ
  • det [[a, b], [c, d]] = ad − bc
  • Circle: (x − h)² + (y − k)² = r²; ellipse: x²/a² + y²/b² = 1; hyperbola: x²/a² − y²/b² = 1

Exam traps

Watch forAB is usually not equal to BA.
Watch forIn (x + 3)² + (y − 2)² = 16, the center is (−3, 2) and the radius is 4, not 16.
Watch forThe dot product is a number, not a vector.

Key terms

  • Parametric function: x and y each given as functions of a parameter t.
  • Eliminating the parameter: Solving for t and substituting to get an x-y equation; the direction of motion is lost.
  • Vector: A quantity with magnitude and direction, written ⟨a, b⟩.
  • Magnitude: ‖⟨a, b⟩‖ = √(a² + b²).
  • Unit vector: A vector of length 1: v ÷ ‖v‖.
  • Dot product: u · v = u₁v₁ + u₂v₂ = ‖u‖‖v‖cos θ.
  • Perpendicular vectors: Their dot product is 0.
  • Matrix multiplication: Row of the first times column of the second; not commutative.
  • Determinant of a 2 × 2 matrix: ad − bc for [[a, b], [c, d]].
  • Inverse of a 2 × 2 matrix: (1/(ad − bc))·[[d, −b], [−c, a]], when ad − bc ≠ 0.
  • Linear transformation: A function of the plane given by a matrix; |det| is the area scale factor.
  • Transition matrix: A matrix whose entries are the probabilities of moving between states.
  • Circle: (x − h)² + (y − k)² = r².
  • Ellipse: x²/a² + y²/b² = 1, with intercepts (±a, 0) and (0, ±b).
  • Hyperbola: x²/a² − y²/b² = 1, with asymptotes y = ±(b/a)x.
  • Parabola (vertex form): y = a(x − h)² + k, vertex (h, k).

Practice questions

Five of the 59 Unit 4 questions in Credit Sprint. Every choice is explained in the app.

1. A particle's motion is x(t) = cos t, y(t) = sin t for 0 ≤ t ≤ 2π. Its path is…
  1. the unit circle, traced counterclockwise once
  2. a parabola
  3. the unit circle, traced clockwise
  4. a line segment
Show answer

the unit circle, traced counterclockwise once. x² + y² = cos²t + sin²t = 1, and the angle t increases, so the motion is counterclockwise.

2. A function f(x) = 3(x − 2)² + 1 restricted to x ≥ 2 has an inverse because…
  1. the restriction makes f one-to-one
  2. the vertex is at (2, 1)
  3. all quadratic functions have inverses
  4. f is odd
Show answer

the restriction makes f one-to-one. On x ≥ 2 the parabola only increases, so it passes the horizontal line test.

3. u = ⟨−1, 3⟩ and v = ⟨1, 4⟩. What is u · v?
  1. 0
  2. 1
  3. 11
  4. ⟨−1, 12⟩
Show answer

11. u · v = (−1)(1) + (3)(4) = 11.

4. u = ⟨2, −2⟩ and v = ⟨−3, 3⟩. What is u · v?
  1. −1
  2. −12
  3. ⟨−6, −6⟩
  4. −13
Show answer

−12. u · v = (2)(−3) + (−2)(3) = −12.

5. What is the determinant of the matrix [[4, −4], [−2, −5]]?
  1. −28
  2. −12
  3. 28
  4. −26
Show answer

−28. det = ad − bc = (4)(−5) − (−4)(−2) = −28.
