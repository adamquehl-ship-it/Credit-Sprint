Credit Sprint's Guide to AP® Calculus AB and BC, Unit 8: Applications of Integration

AB weight 10–15%BC weight 5–10%CLEP® about 10% (no volumes)

Unit 8 applies the definite integral: average value, position and distance from velocity, accumulation in context, area between curves, and volumes of solids with known cross sections and solids of revolution (disc and washer methods).

What you need to know

  • Average value of f on [a, b]: f_avg = [1 ÷ (b − a)]·∫ₐᵇ f(x) dx. It is the height of the rectangle with the same area.
  • From velocity: displacement = ∫ₐᵇ v(t) dt, position s(b) = s(a) + ∫ₐᵇ v(t) dt, and total distance traveled = ∫ₐᵇ |v(t)| dt.
  • Accumulation in context: amount at time t = initial amount + ∫ (rate in − rate out) dt. Rates in and out often come as separate functions.
  • Area between two curves: ∫ₐᵇ [top − bottom] dx with vertical slices, or ∫ [right − left] dy with horizontal slices. Find intersection points for the limits.
  • If the curves cross inside the interval, split the integral at each crossing, or integrate the absolute value of the difference.
  • Volume with known cross sections: V = ∫ₐᵇ A(x) dx, where A(x) is the area of a cross section. Squares: A = s²; semicircles: A = (π/8)s²; equilateral triangles: A = (√3/4)s²; isosceles right triangles with a leg on the base: A = ½s².
  • Disc method (revolving around a horizontal line): V = π∫ₐᵇ [R(x)]² dx, where R is the distance from the curve to the axis.
  • Washer method: V = π∫ₐᵇ ([R(x)]² − [r(x)]²) dx, with R the outer radius and r the inner radius, both measured from the axis of revolution.
  • Revolving around a line other than an axis, such as y = −2 or x = 3, changes the radii: R = (distance from the axis to the farther curve), r = (distance to the nearer curve).
  • (BC) Arc length of y = f(x) from a to b: L = ∫ₐᵇ √(1 + [f′(x)]²) dx. Most arc-length integrals on the exam need a calculator.

Formulas

  • f_avg = 1/(b − a) · ∫ₐᵇ f(x) dx
  • Distance = ∫ₐᵇ |v(t)| dt
  • Area = ∫ₐᵇ (top − bottom) dx
  • Washer: V = π∫ₐᵇ (R² − r²) dx
  • Cross sections: V = ∫ₐᵇ A(x) dx
  • Arc length: ∫ₐᵇ √(1 + (f′(x))²) dx

Exam traps

Watch forIn the washer method, it is R² − r², not (R − r)². Square each radius first.
Watch forAverage value of a function is not the average rate of change. Average value uses an integral; average rate of change uses a difference quotient.
Watch forWhen revolving around y = −2, the radius is f(x) − (−2) = f(x) + 2.
Watch forTotal distance needs the absolute value of velocity. Split the integral where v changes sign.
Watch forArea between curves is always positive. If you get a negative number, top and bottom are switched.

Key terms

  • Average value: f_avg = [1/(b − a)]·∫ₐᵇ f(x) dx.
  • Displacement: ∫ₐᵇ v(t) dt: the net change in position.
  • Total distance: ∫ₐᵇ |v(t)| dt: split where v changes sign.
  • Position from velocity: s(b) = s(a) + ∫ₐᵇ v(t) dt.
  • Amount from rates in and out: A(t) = A(0) + ∫₀ᵗ [rate in − rate out] dt.
  • Area between curves (vertical slices): ∫ₐᵇ [top − bottom] dx.
  • Area between curves (horizontal slices): ∫ [right − left] dy.
  • Known cross sections: V = ∫ₐᵇ A(x) dx, with A(x) the area of a cross section.
  • Square cross section: A = s², where s is the side in the base.
  • Semicircle cross section: A = (π/8)s², where s is the diameter in the base.
  • Equilateral triangle cross section: A = (√3/4)s².
  • Disc method: V = π∫ₐᵇ [R(x)]² dx.
  • Washer method: V = π∫ₐᵇ ([R(x)]² − [r(x)]²) dx.
  • Radius about another line: Distance from the axis of revolution to the curve, such as f(x) + 2 for the axis y = −2.
  • Arc length: L = ∫ₐᵇ √(1 + [f′(x)]²) dx.

Practice questions

Five of the 57 Unit 8 questions in Credit Sprint. Every choice is explained in the app.

1. v(t) is a particle's velocity. Which integral gives the total distance traveled from t = 0 to t = 6?
  1. ∫ from 0 to 6 of v′(t) dt
  2. ∫ from 0 to 6 of v(t) dt
  3. ∫ from 0 to 6 of |v(t)| dt
  4. v(6) − v(0)
Show answer

∫ from 0 to 6 of |v(t)| dt. Distance counts motion in both directions as positive, so integrate speed.

2. A particle starts at x = 3 and has velocity v(t). Where is it at t = 5?
  1. 3 + ∫ from 0 to 5 of v(t) dt
  2. ∫ from 0 to 5 of v(t) dt
  3. 3 + ∫ from 0 to 5 of |v(t)| dt
  4. 3 + v(5)
Show answer

3 + ∫ from 0 to 5 of v(t) dt. Position = starting position + displacement.

3. What is the average value of f(x) = −3x² + 4x + 1 on [0, 4]?
  1. −7
  2. −8
  3. −28
  4. −15
Show answer

−7. f_avg = [1/(4 − 0)]·∫ f(x) dx = (1/4)(−28) = −7.

4. What is the area of the region enclosed by y = −x² + 7x − 10 and y = x − 2?
  1. 8/3
  2. 10/3
  3. −4/3
  4. 4/3
Show answer

4/3. The curves meet where −x² + 7x − 10 = x − 2, at x = 2 and x = 4. Area = ∫ from 2 to 4 of [(−x² + 7x − 10) − (x − 2)] dx = 4/3.

5. The region under y = √x from x = 0 to x = 4 is revolved about the x-axis. What is the volume of the solid?
  1. 5.33π
  2. 8π
  3. 16π
  4. 8
Show answer

8π. Disc method: V = π∫ from 0 to 4 of (√x)² dx = π∫ x dx = 8π.
