Credit Sprint's Guide to AP® Calculus AB and BC, Unit 7: Differential Equations

AB weight 5–10%BC weight 5–10%CLEP® about 5%

Unit 7 is about equations that involve a derivative. You verify solutions, sketch and read slope fields, solve separable differential equations with initial conditions, and model exponential growth and decay.

What you need to know

  • A differential equation relates a function to its derivatives, such as dy/dx = x·y. A solution is a function that makes the equation true; verify one by substituting it and its derivative.
  • A slope field draws a short segment with slope dy/dx at each point. Solution curves follow the segments. Where dy/dx depends only on x, segments in each vertical column match; where it depends only on y, segments in each row match.
  • To match a slope field to an equation, check where the slopes are zero (horizontal segments) and where they are positive or negative.
  • Separation of variables: rewrite as g(y) dy = f(x) dx, integrate both sides, add one + C, use the initial condition to find C, then solve for y. Do this in that order.
  • The general solution has + C (a family of curves); a particular solution uses the initial condition to pin down C.
  • Exponential models: if dy/dt = ky, then y = y₀·e^(kt). k > 0 is growth, k < 0 is decay. The rate of change is proportional to the amount present.
  • Use the differential equation itself to answer questions about increasing, decreasing and concavity of a solution: the sign of dy/dx gives direction; differentiate dy/dx (implicitly) to get d²y/dx² for concavity.
  • The tangent line to a solution at the initial point gives a quick approximation: y ≈ y₀ + (dy/dx at the point)(x − x₀).
  • (BC) Euler's method approximates a solution step by step: yₙ₊₁ = yₙ + (dy/dx at (xₙ, yₙ))·Δx. Each step follows the tangent line for one step size.
  • (BC) Logistic growth: dP/dt = kP(1 − P/L). The population approaches the carrying capacity L, grows fastest when P = L/2 (the inflection point of the solution), and lim P(t) as t → ∞ is L when P(0) > 0.

Formulas

  • Separable: ∫g(y) dy = ∫f(x) dx
  • dy/dt = ky ⇒ y = y₀e^(kt)
  • Half-life: t = ln 2 / |k|
  • Euler: yₙ₊₁ = yₙ + f(xₙ, yₙ)·Δx
  • Logistic: dP/dt = kP(1 − P/L), fastest growth at P = L/2

Exam traps

Watch forSeparate the variables completely before integrating. You cannot integrate x·y dx with respect to x when y is a function of x.
Watch forAdd + C right after integrating, then use the initial condition before solving for y. Adding it at the end gives the wrong function.
Watch forWhen you take ln of both sides, ln|y| = … gives y = ±e^(…). The initial condition picks the sign.
Watch forA slope field shows slopes, not values of y. Do not read a segment's height as the solution.
Watch for(BC) In Euler's method, use the slope at the start of each step, not the end. Recompute the slope after every step.

Key terms

  • Differential equation: An equation involving a function and its derivatives, such as dy/dx = 3xy.
  • Verifying a solution: Substitute the function and its derivative; the equation must hold.
  • Slope field: A grid of short segments with slope dy/dx at each point; solution curves follow them.
  • Separation of variables: Rewrite as g(y) dy = f(x) dx, integrate both sides, use the initial condition, solve for y.
  • General vs. particular solution: General: a family with + C. Particular: the one that meets the initial condition.
  • Exponential growth and decay: dy/dt = ky ⇒ y = y₀e^(kt); k > 0 grows, k < 0 decays.
  • Half-life: The time for an amount to halve: t = ln 2 / |k|.
  • Proportional to the amount: 'The rate of change of y is proportional to y' means dy/dt = ky.
  • Euler's method: yₙ₊₁ = yₙ + (dy/dx at (xₙ, yₙ))·Δx: follow tangent lines step by step.
  • Logistic differential equation: dP/dt = kP(1 − P/L), with carrying capacity L.
  • Carrying capacity: The limiting value L of a logistic model: P(t) → L as t → ∞.
  • Fastest logistic growth: When P = L/2, the inflection point of the logistic curve.

Practice questions

Five of the 79 Unit 7 questions in Credit Sprint. Every choice is explained in the app.

1. Which function is a solution to the differential equation dy/dx = 2y?
  1. y = 5e^(2x)
  2. y = e^(x²)
  3. y = 2x + 5
  4. y = x²
Show answer

y = 5e^(2x). dy/dx = 10e^(2x) = 2(5e^(2x)) = 2y.

2. A slope field shows horizontal segments along the line y = x. Which equation could it be?
  1. dy/dx = y − x
  2. dy/dx = x + y
  3. dy/dx = xy
  4. dy/dx = x
Show answer

dy/dx = y − x. The slope is 0 exactly where y = x.

3. A population grows according to dP/dt = kP. It starts at 100 and is 300 after 5 years. What is the population after 15 years?
  1. 2700
  2. 900
  3. 8100
  4. 700
Show answer

2700. P = 100e^(kt), and e^(5k) = 3. After 15 years, P = 100·(e^(5k))^3 = 100·3³ = 2700.

4. For the differential equation dy/dx = x + y, what is the slope of the solution curve through the point (3, 1)?
  1. 1
  2. 3
  3. 4
  4. 5
Show answer

4. Substitute x = 3 and y = 1 into x + y: the slope is 4.

5. For the differential equation dy/dx = x² − y, what is the slope of the solution curve through the point (3, 2)?
  1. 1
  2. 2
  3. 7
  4. 8
Show answer

7. Substitute x = 3 and y = 2 into x² − y: the slope is 7.
