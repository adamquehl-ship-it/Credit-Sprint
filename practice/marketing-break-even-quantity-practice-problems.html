Break-even quantity practice problems

Problem 1.

A firm has fixed costs of $30,000 per year. Its product sells for $35 and the variable cost is $25 per unit. How many units must it sell to break even? (Round up to a whole unit.)

Show solution

Contribution per unit = 35 − 25 = 10. Break-even = 30000 ÷ 10 = 3000, so 3000 units.
