U.S. Government. Try each one, then open the answer. The Credit Sprint app generates unlimited new versions.

Item 1.

In an election, 1,440,000 of 2,000,000 eligible voters cast ballots. What is the turnout percentage, rounded to the nearest whole number?

Show answer

1,440,000 ÷ 2,000,000 = 72.0%.
