Doubling time: practice

Human Geography Unit 2. Try each one, then open the answer. The Credit Sprint app generates unlimited new versions.

Problem 1.

A population is growing at 0.5% per year. Using the rule of 70, about how many years will it take to double?

Show answer

Doubling time ≈ 70 ÷ growth rate = 70 ÷ 0.5 ≈ 140 years.
