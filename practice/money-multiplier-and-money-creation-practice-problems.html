Problem 1.

A customer deposits $5,500 in cash. The reserve requirement is 25%. What is the maximum increase in the money supply from new loans across the banking system?

Show solution

Excess reserves = $5,500 × 0.75 = $4,125. Multiplier = 4. $4,125 × 4 = $16,500.
