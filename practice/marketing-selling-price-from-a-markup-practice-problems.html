Selling price from a markup practice problems

Marketing Unit 8. Try each one, then open the worked solution. The Credit Sprint app generates unlimited new versions with fresh numbers.

Problem 1.

A retailer pays $180 for a coffee maker and adds a markup of 75% on cost. What is the selling price in dollars?

Show solution

Markup = 75% × 180 = 135. Selling price = 180 + 135 = 315.

Problem 2.

Show solution
