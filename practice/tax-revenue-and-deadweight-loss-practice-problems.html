Tax revenue and deadweight loss practice problems

Microeconomics Unit 2. Try each one, then open the worked solution. The Credit Sprint app generates unlimited new versions with fresh numbers.

Problem 1.

Before a $3 per-unit tax, 330 units are sold. After the tax, 280 units are sold. What is the government's tax revenue?

Show solution

Revenue = $3 × 280 = $840.

Problem 2.

Before a $2 per-unit tax, 190 units are sold. After the tax, 130 units are sold. What is the government's tax revenue?

Show solution

Revenue = $2 × 130 = $260.

Problem 3.

Before a $5 per-unit tax, 110 units are sold. After the tax, 80 units are sold. What is the government's tax revenue?

Show solution

Revenue = $5 × 80 = $400.

Problem 4.

Before a $2 per-unit tax, 200 units are sold. After the tax, 180 units are sold. What is the government's tax revenue?

Show solution

Revenue = $2 × 180 = $360.

Problem 5.

Before a $2 per-unit tax, 350 units are sold. After the tax, 280 units are sold. What is the government's tax revenue?

Show solution

Revenue = $2 × 280 = $560.

Problem 6.

Before a $3 per-unit tax, 400 units are sold. After the tax, 330 units are sold. What is the deadweight loss?

Show solution

DWL = ½ × $3 × (400 − 330) = $105.
