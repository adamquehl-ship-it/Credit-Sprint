Microeconomics Unit 3. Try each one, then open the worked solution. The Credit Sprint app generates unlimited new versions with fresh numbers.

Problem 1.

A firm's fixed cost is $130 and its variable cost of 20 units is $80. What is average total cost at 20 units?

Show solution

ATC = ($130 + $80) ÷ 20 = $10.5.
