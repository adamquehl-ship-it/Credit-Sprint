Problem 1.

A store changes its price from $28 to $29, and sales go from 780 to 710 units. By how much does total revenue change? (Use a negative number for a decrease.)

Show solution

New TR = $29 × 710 = $20,590. Old TR = $28 × 780 = $21,840. Change = $-1,250. Price and TR moved in opposite directions, so demand is elastic here.

Problem 2.

A store changes its price from $6 to $5, and sales go from 890 to 990 units. By how much does total revenue change? (Use a negative number for a decrease.)

Show solution
