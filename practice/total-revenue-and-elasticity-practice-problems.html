Total revenue and elasticity practice problems

Problem 1.

A store changes its price from $29 to $30, and sales go from 420 to 330 units. By how much does total revenue change? (Use a negative number for a decrease.)

Show solution

New TR = $30 × 330 = $9,900. Old TR = $29 × 420 = $12,180. Change = $-2,280. Price and TR moved in opposite directions, so demand is elastic here.

Problem 2.

A store changes its price from $21 to $22, and sales go from 860 to 840 units. By how much does total revenue change? (Use a negative number for a decrease.)

Show solution

New TR = $22 × 840 = $18,480. Old TR = $21 × 860 = $18,060. Change = $420. Price and TR moved together, so demand is inelastic here.
