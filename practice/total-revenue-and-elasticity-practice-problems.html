Total revenue and elasticity practice problems

Problem 1.

A store changes its price from $22 to $19, and sales go from 220 to 260 units. By how much does total revenue change? (Use a negative number for a decrease.)

Show solution

New TR = $19 × 260 = $4,940. Old TR = $22 × 220 = $4,840. Change = $100. Price and TR moved in opposite directions, so demand is elastic here.

Problem 2.

A store changes its price from $6 to $9, and sales go from 570 to 520 units. By how much does total revenue change? (Use a negative number for a decrease.)

Show solution

New TR = $9 × 520 = $4,680. Old TR = $6 × 570 = $3,420. Change = $1,260. Price and TR moved together, so demand is inelastic here.
