Problem 1.

A store changes its price from $11 to $12, and sales go from 240 to 90 units. By how much does total revenue change? (Use a negative number for a decrease.)

Show solution

New TR = $12 × 90 = $1,080. Old TR = $11 × 240 = $2,640. Change = $-1,560. Price and TR moved in opposite directions, so demand is elastic here.
