Problem 1.

A population has r = 0.4, N = 125 and carrying capacity K = 500. Use dN/dt = rN(K − N)/K. What is dN/dt?

Show solution

0.4 × 125 × (500 − 125) ÷ 500 = 37.5 per unit time.
