Molarity practice problems

Chemistry Unit 4. Try each one, then open the worked solution. The Credit Sprint app generates unlimited new versions with fresh numbers.

Problem 1.

15 g of H₂SO₄ (98.08 g/mol) is dissolved to make 0.5 L of solution. What is the molarity?

Show solution

n = 15 ÷ 98.08 = 0.153 mol; M = n ÷ V = 0.153 ÷ 0.5 = 0.306 M.

Problem 2.

40 g of H₂O (18.02 g/mol) is dissolved to make 2 L of solution. What is the molarity?

Show solution

n = 40 ÷ 18.02 = 2.22 mol; M = n ÷ V = 2.22 ÷ 2 = 1.11 M.
