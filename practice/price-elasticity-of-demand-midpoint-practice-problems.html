Problem 1.

When the price of concert tickets rises from $5 to $8, quantity demanded falls from 600 to 560. Using the midpoint method, what is the price elasticity of demand? (Round to 2 decimals.)

Show solution

%ΔQ = -40 ÷ 580 = -6.9%. %ΔP = 3 ÷ 6.5 = 46.15%. |-6.9 ÷ 46.15| = 0.15. Inelastic.
