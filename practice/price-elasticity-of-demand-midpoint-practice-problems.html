Problem 1.

When the price of concert tickets rises from $6 to $10, quantity demanded falls from 550 to 330. Using the midpoint method, what is the price elasticity of demand? (Round to 2 decimals.)

Show solution

%ΔQ = -220 ÷ 440 = -50%. %ΔP = 4 ÷ 8 = 50%. |-50 ÷ 50| = 1. Unit elastic.
