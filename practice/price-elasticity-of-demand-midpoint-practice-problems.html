Problem 1.

When the price of concert tickets rises from $13 to $15, quantity demanded falls from 310 to 280. Using the midpoint method, what is the price elasticity of demand? (Round to 2 decimals.)

Show solution

%ΔQ = -30 ÷ 295 = -10.17%. %ΔP = 2 ÷ 14 = 14.29%. |-10.17 ÷ 14.29| = 0.71. Inelastic.
