Problem 1.

When the price of concert tickets rises from $12 to $15, quantity demanded falls from 420 to 250. Using the midpoint method, what is the price elasticity of demand? (Round to 2 decimals.)

Show solution

%ΔQ = -170 ÷ 335 = -50.75%. %ΔP = 3 ÷ 13.5 = 22.22%. |-50.75 ÷ 22.22| = 2.28. Elastic.
