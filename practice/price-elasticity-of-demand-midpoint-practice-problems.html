Problem 1.

When the price of concert tickets rises from $18 to $19, quantity demanded falls from 170 to 120. Using the midpoint method, what is the price elasticity of demand? (Round to 2 decimals.)

Show solution

%ΔQ = -50 ÷ 145 = -34.48%. %ΔP = 1 ÷ 18.5 = 5.41%. |-34.48 ÷ 5.41| = 6.38. Elastic.
