Problem 1.

When the price of concert tickets rises from $5 to $7, quantity demanded falls from 380 to 290. Using the midpoint method, what is the price elasticity of demand? (Round to 2 decimals.)

Show solution

%ΔQ = -90 ÷ 335 = -26.87%. %ΔP = 2 ÷ 6 = 33.33%. |-26.87 ÷ 33.33| = 0.81. Inelastic.
